The meaning behind the name

Our Story

AJ Legacy is inspired by Andrew John Faught, known as Drew, who served with the Illinois Department of Corrections and was a member of its Tactical Response Team.

His legacy is the foundation of a brand dedicated to honoring service, perseverance, and the people who stand beside one another.

Our Mission

AJ Legacy Apparel aims to build something meaningful through quality merchandise, community, and remembrance.

Our planned commitment is to donate 5% of net profits to C.O.P.S. (Concerns of Police Survivors), subject to final business arrangements.
