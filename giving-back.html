What C.O.P.S. does

Concerns of Police Survivors supports families and coworkers after a law enforcement officer dies in the line of duty. Local chapters provide peer support, help survivors navigate trials, and connect families with survivor weekends, camps, and National Police Week.

Learn about C.O.P.S. and its local chapters

What that support meant to our family

C.O.P.S. helped our family travel to National Police Week in Washington, D.C., and made sure we were cared for throughout the experience. We were able to visit the Fallen Officers Wall, see Drew’s name there, and remember him together.
