Returns & Refunds

Online ordering is currently closed. These return instructions apply once sales begin.

Request a return within 30 days of delivery for standard apparel that is unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition, with tags attached if supplied.

Personalized items are final sale for change-of-mind or fit returns. If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us. We cover return shipping when a return is needed for one of these issues.

To start a return, email ajlegacyapparelsupport@gmail.com with your order number, the item, and the reason for the return. For damaged, defective, or incorrect items, include photos if you can. Wait for our return address and instructions before mailing anything back.

Customers cover return shipping for standard returns. We issue approved refunds to the original payment method after the return is received and inspected. We refund original shipping charges for damaged, defective, or incorrect items, or when required by law.
